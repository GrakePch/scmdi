## 2. Icon artwork

SCMDI catalog icons are provided under Apache-2.0 with their source and
attribution records retained. The catalog includes original artwork, unmodified
Material Design Icons (MDI), modified MDI artwork, and adaptations using
Star Citizen in-game assets as reference. Recorded upstream names, available
author credits and modification notices are provided in the website's icon
details and in comments next to the package's path exports.

MDI artwork retains its upstream Apache-2.0 license. Unmodified references use
SC-specific names; their underlying artwork remains credited to its original
contributors. Modified references include camera-outline, eye, car-light-high,
timer-outline and account. They carry a notice in the form:

    Modified from MDI <upstream-name> for SCMDI.

TurretManned retains both its game-asset reference and its modified MDI account
component. Interface icons also use MDI paths. Applicable upstream copyright,
license and attribution notices remain attached to their respective components.
